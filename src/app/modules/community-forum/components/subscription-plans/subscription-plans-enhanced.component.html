❓ Frequently Asked Questions

▶ What is the difference between Basic and Premium plans?

Basic Plan (Free):

  • Access to public forum
  • Responses limited to 5 per month
  • No priority support
  • No access to premium resources

Premium Plan:

  • Unlimited discussions
  • Priority expert responses
  • Priority support
  • Access to all exclusive content
  • Annual Plan: Video consultations + Webinars
▶ Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription anytime without penalty. Your access will end immediately at the end of the current billing period. No questions asked!

▶ Is there a free trial period?

We offer 7 days of free access to the Premium Plan for new users. During this period, you can cancel free of charge if you are not satisfied.

▶ Can I change plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime. If you switch to a cheaper plan, your credit will be applied to your next invoice.

▶ What payment methods are accepted?

We accept:

  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Bank transfers

All payments are secure and encrypted.

▶ Does the premium plan really include video consultations?

Yes! The Annual Premium Plan includes:

  • ✓ Up to 4 video consultations per year (1 per quarter)
  • ✓ Duration: 30 minutes each
  • ✓ With qualified doctors or specialists
  • ✓ Flexible scheduling based on your availability
▶ How does auto-renewal work?

Auto-renewal is enabled by default when you purchase a subscription. Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing period. You can disable it anytime from your account, and we'll remind you before renewal.
