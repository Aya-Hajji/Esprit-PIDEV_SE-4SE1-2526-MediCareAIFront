Ambulatory scenario (reference)
  1. Identity — contacts and dossier lifecycle (active / archived).
  2. Allergies — documented before any medication (alert generation).
  3. Problems & treatments — longitudinal conditions.
  4. Encounter note — SOAP-style documentation of this visit.
  5. Labs & imaging — biological and morphological evidence.
  6. E‑prescribing — after allergy reconciliation.
  7. Telehealth — synchronous session when scheduled.
  8. Decision support — risk, referrals, assistant.
